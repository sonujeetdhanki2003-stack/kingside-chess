Install on Android

Takes about a minute. The app isn’t on the Play Store, so Android will ask you once to allow it.

  1. Tap Download for Android. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file from the notification, or from Files → Downloads.
  3. If you see “installs from this source aren’t allowed”, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open. Done!
Play Protect warning?

Google Play Protect may say the app is unknown, because it isn’t from the Play Store. Tap More details → Install anyway. If it offers to scan the app, you can let it. Every version is signed with the same key, so updates install over the top.

Install on Windows

A real Windows app with its own window, Start menu entry and desktop shortcut. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

  1. Click Download for Windows (109 MB).
  2. Open KingsideChess-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  3. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Click Next and Install. Kingside Chess opens when it’s done, and it’s on your desktop and in the Start menu.
“Unknown publisher” warning?

Windows SmartScreen shows this for apps that aren’t from the Microsoft Store and don’t have a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info → Run anyway. The app plays the computer offline, and online games, friends and tournaments are shared with Android and browser players.

Questions

Short answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Playing the computer, playing a friend on the same device and Game Review work with no internet in the Android and Windows apps, because the engine runs on your device. Online games, Quick Match, friends and tournaments need a connection.

Is there a PC version?

Yes, for Windows 10 and 11. Click Download for Windows and follow these steps. On a Mac or Linux, or if you’d rather not install, use the web version; in Chrome or Edge you can install it from the address bar.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. Open the web version in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It works like an app, and online games are shared with Android players.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play everything as a guest. An account (just a username and password, no email) keeps your rating, puzzle progress and game history, and lets you add friends.

I forgot my password.

When you sign up you get 6 one-time recovery codes. On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password” and enter one to set a new password. Keep the codes somewhere safe.

How strong is the computer?

It has 10 levels, from Beginner (about 250 rating) to Maximum (3000+), which is full-strength Stockfish 19 and stronger than any human. Levels 1 to 5 play like real people, with natural moves and the occasional mistake, so they’re great for learning.

How do I update?

Download the new version from this page and install it over the old one. Your games and settings stay.

Privacy

There are no ads, no trackers and no analytics. Guest games stay on your phone. If you make an account, the server stores your username, a securely hashed password, your ratings, your games and your friends list. We never ask for your email or phone number. Chat messages are kept with their game. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Profile → Settings.
